## BEAT 1: Get a transcript first ([__] min)

Before I open the editor, I get a transcript. I use [FREE TRANSCRIPTION TOOL]. A transcript lets me read the video instead of watching it, and reading is a lot faster than scrubbing.

[ON SCREEN: Raw file dropped into [FREE TRANSCRIPTION TOOL]. The transcript scrolls by. Timer in the corner.]

## BEAT 2: The rough cut, from the text ([__] min)

Now I cut from the transcript. Every retake, every false start, every "um, so, yeah": gone. If I said something twice, I keep the better take and delete the other one without rewatching both. This is the step that eats a weekend if you do it by eye.

[ON SCREEN: Transcript with retakes highlighted in red, then the matching gaps closing on the timeline in [FREE EDITOR].]

## BEAT 3: The structure pass ([__] min)

Then I watch the rough cut once, start to finish, and ask one question: where would I click away? Wherever the answer is "here", I cut harder or move that section. I don't fix anything else on this pass. Only structure.

[ON SCREEN: The rough cut playing at full size, with a marker dropping on the timeline at each "click away" moment.]

## BEAT 4: Lock the cut

This is the rule the whole hour depends on. Once the structure pass is done, the cut is locked. No more trimming. Everything after this is polish, and polish only happens once.

[ON SCREEN: The word "LOCKED" stamped over the timeline. The timer pauses for a beat.]
