Score key: S=Specificity, A=Address, St=Stakes, C=Curiosity, B=Brevity. The verdict is 60% the average plus 40% the weakest of the five.
STRONG ≥72, WORKABLE 55–71, WEAK <55. The scorer is a heuristic: a low score is a reason to look again, and a high score is not a promise.
