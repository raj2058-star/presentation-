Built with the `/yt-plan` shape: **one anchor** (a flagship, every other Tuesday), **one cheap one** (in the week between),
and **three Shorts a week** cut from the anchor. Never seven uploads a week.

**Assumed capacity: about 20 hours a week, one person.** If you have less, see "What to drop first" at the bottom.

## ⚠️ Monetization: plan for the new bar

Reported by several outlets, but **confirm in Studio → Earn**:
- **Until 31 Jan 2027:** 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 watch hours in 12 months, *or* 10M Shorts views in 90 days.
- **From 1 Feb 2027 (new applicants):** 1,000 subscribers plus **8,000** watch hours, *or* **20M** Shorts views in 90 days.
- **A lower tier for fan funding** (memberships, Super Thanks): 500 subscribers, 3 uploads in 90 days, plus 3,000 hours or 3M Shorts views (region-dependent).

A brand-new channel realistically won't hit the old bar by 31 January, so **plan for 8,000 hours**.
Long-form watch time is the route that suits this channel, and **the marathons are your biggest
watch-hour builder**. They're also why they're on the calendar. Shorts watch time doesn't count toward the hours.

## 📊 The reviews

**At 3 months (week of 1 Mar)** and **at 6 months (week of 24 May)**, run:
1. **`/yt-audit`** on the channel. It ends with *one* fix, not twenty.
2. **`/yt-viral`** again on your own videos (`swipe.py` with your channel's data) to find your own outliers.
3. Sort all flagships by day-7 views against the channel median. **The top two pillars get two-thirds of the next quarter's slots.**
4. Check the hook leak across all videos (`/yt-retention`). If the average is above 25%, the next quarter's single focus is the first 15 seconds.

There are **no promised view or subscriber targets** here. Nobody can honestly forecast them for a new channel.
The gates are relative: is each video beating the channel's own median, and is that median rising?

## The cheap ones (in the weeks between flagships)

Each should take **under 5 hours** and be built from something that already exists:
- **Comment follow-ups:** answer the most-asked question from the last flagship (`/yt-comment` finds the repeats)
- **Ledger updates:** "this forecast just moved," using a new report or a launch date that changed
- **New-year Ledger list** (5 Jan)
- **Short explainers (5 minutes)** expanded from a Short that did unusually well

## Hours and what to drop first

About **19 hours a week** for the flagship cycle, plus about 3 for Shorts, plus about 2.5 for the cheap ones (averaged), **so about 24 hours a week**.

If a week goes wrong, drop things in this order:
1. **The cheap one.** Skip it, or replace it with a Community post.
2. **Shorts, down to 2 a week.**
3. **Never the flagship.** If the flagship is at risk, use the buffer week (12 Apr) or publish it a week late. Don't ship it half-finished.
