# 07: Image prompts for ChatGPT (every picture the channel needs)

Written for **ChatGPT's image generator**. Paste each prompt into ChatGPT as an ordinary message.

---

## How to use these with ChatGPT (read once)

**1. Start a new chat just for brand images, and send this style message first.** It sets the look for every image you
make in that chat:

> For this whole conversation, I'm making images for a faceless YouTube documentary channel called "Tomorrow's Record".
> Use one consistent house style in every image: flat vector illustration with subtle depth, minimalist editorial look,
> a deep navy blue background (hex #0B1426), glowing amber accents (hex #FFB020), warm off-white highlights (hex #F4F1EA),
> high contrast, clean geometric shapes, a soft amber glow, and a fine film-grain texture. Never show human faces. Use
> silhouettes or people seen from behind instead. Don't add any text, logos or watermarks unless I put the exact words
> in quotes. When I give you text in quotes, spell it exactly, in a bold, clean, geometric sans-serif font, all caps.
> Reply "Ready" and wait for my first image request.

**2. Then send the prompts below one at a time**, in the same chat. If one drifts off-style, reply:
*"Same image, but match the house style more closely: darker navy, more amber glow."*

**3. Fix things by talking to it.** For example: *"Move the text to the left"*, *"Make the text bigger"*, *"Remove the glow on the right"*, *"Spell it exactly: AM I SAFE?"*.

**4. Image sizes and cropping.** ChatGPT makes **square (1:1)**, **wide (3:2)** or **tall (2:3)** images. It can't make the 16:9 that YouTube uses, so:
- The wide prompts say *"keep everything important in the middle band"*.
- In **Canva**: Resize → 1280×720 (thumbnails) or 2560×1440 (banner) → drag the image to fit. Only the top and bottom edges get cropped.

**5. Check the spelling** in every image with text. ChatGPT is usually right on short words, but not always. If it's wrong, ask again, or delete the text in Canva and type it yourself.

## Three rules that don't change

- **Charts and numbers are never AI.** Every bar, counter and graph in the videos is built in Canva or your editor from the real source numbers.
- **Don't fake real places.** The "being built" episodes (EP04, EP07, EP10) use **real satellite images** (Copernicus Sentinel, NASA Earth Observatory) inside the video. The AI art is for thumbnails and illustrations, and it's kept stylized so nobody mistakes it for a photo.
- **If you ever make something photorealistic** of a real person, place or event, switch on YouTube's altered content label when uploading.

# A. Brand kit

### A1. Profile picture / logo: square

> Make a square logo image. A minimalist circular emblem: a thin glowing amber ring, with a short amber horizontal line
> crossing through its center like a timeline, ending in a small bright amber dot. Inside the ring, the letters "TR" in a
> bold geometric sans-serif, off-white. Solid deep navy background. Perfectly centered and symmetrical, with lots of empty
> space around the emblem, because it will be cropped into a circle and shown very small. Flat vector, no other text.

**Option without letters:** send the same prompt with *"Don't put any letters inside the ring"*.
**Check:** zoom out until it's thumbnail-size. If the ring or the letters get lost, ask for *"a thicker ring and bolder letters"*.

### A2. Channel banner: wide

> Make a wide landscape image for a YouTube channel banner. The curved horizon of planet Earth seen from orbit at night, in
> deep navy, with tiny amber dots of city lights. A single thin glowing amber timeline line arcs across the whole image from
> left to right just above the horizon, with small evenly spaced tick marks. A faint sunrise glow on the far right edge. In the
> exact center, the text "TOMORROW'S RECORD" in large bold off-white letters, and directly under it, smaller amber text:
> "THE FUTURE, ON THE RECORD". Keep the text and everything important inside a narrow horizontal strip across the middle of
> the image, because the top, bottom and sides get cropped on phones.

**Then in Canva:** resize to 2560×1440 and check the text sits inside the center 1546×423 safe area. Add "New documentary every other Tuesday" there if you like.

### A3. Watermark

Don't generate a new one. Take the A1 logo into Canva → resize to 150×150 → Download → PNG → tick **transparent background**.

### A4. The five topic icons (all five in one image, so they match)

> Make a wide image showing a set of five matching icons in one row, evenly spaced, each inside its own rounded navy square
> tile. Same style for all five: thick rounded amber line art with one small off-white accent, flat vector, simple bold
> shapes readable at small size. From left to right: (1) a briefcase with a small circuit-board pattern inside, (2) a
> construction crane next to three simple skyscraper outlines, (3) a wind turbine with a sun half-rising behind it, (4) a
> rocket on a curved path around a small moon, (5) a coin breaking apart into small square pixels on one side. No text.

**Then:** crop each icon out in Canva. They're for the playlists, the banner and the topic sections.

### A5. Thumbnail background: wide

> Make a wide, empty background for YouTube thumbnails. A deep navy gradient getting darker toward the edges, a soft amber
> glow coming from the right third of the image, a very faint fine grid of thin lines fading into the dark, and fine film
> grain. No objects and no text. Keep the left half calm and empty for text.

**Then:** in Canva, save it as a template with an amber year stamp in the top-left (for example "2035").

### A6. Ledger card: wide

> Make a wide image of a single paper index card lying flat, seen straight from above, in the center of a solid deep navy
> background. Warm off-white paper with a subtle texture, slightly rounded corners, a thin amber border down the left edge,
> and a soft shadow. At the top-left of the card, the text "LEDGER #0001" in small bold navy letters. Below that, four
> short, thin, empty lines labelled "CLAIM", "SOURCE", "DATE SAID", "CHECK DATE" in tiny grey letters. The rest of the card
> is empty.

**Then:** in Canva, delete the AI text and type it yourself for each entry, so the numbers and claims are exact.

### A7. Status stamps (all three in one image)

> Make a wide image with three round rubber ink stamps side by side on a plain white background, seen from above, with
> slightly imperfect ink texture like real stamps. Left: a green stamp (#30A46C) with the word "RIGHT". Middle: an amber
> stamp (#FFB020) with the word "PARTLY". Right: a red stamp (#E5484D) with the word "WRONG". Each has a bold double circular
> border, with the word across the center in bold capital letters. Nothing else in the image.

**Then:** remove the white background in Canva (Edit → BG Remover) and crop each stamp out.

### A8. End screen background: wide

> Make a wide, calm end-screen background. Deep navy, a thin glowing amber timeline line running horizontally across the lower
> third, a faint starfield, and a soft amber glow in the top-right corner. Leave large empty areas on the left and the right,
> because video thumbnails will be placed there. In small off-white letters at the bottom center: "TOMORROW'S RECORD".

### A9. Source label strip

Make it in Canva, not with AI: a navy rounded rectangle at 80% opacity with an amber left edge, and the text `Source: WEF 2025` in a clean sans-serif.
