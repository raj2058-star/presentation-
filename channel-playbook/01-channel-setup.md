## Step 2: Accounts and security (day 1)

1. Create a **new Google account** just for the channel, so it's not your personal Gmail.
2. Turn on **2-Step Verification** with an authenticator app or a passkey, not SMS alone. Channels get stolen through phishing emails pretending to be sponsors.
3. Go to youtube.com → profile picture → **Create a channel**, and enter the custom name and handle above.
4. **YouTube Studio → Settings → Channel → Feature eligibility:**
   - **Intermediate features (phone verification):** unlocks **custom thumbnails** and **videos longer than 15 minutes**. Required.
   - **Advanced features (video or ID verification, or channel history):** unlocks higher limits and more features. Do it now.
5. Create a separate business email (e.g. `hello@` on a cheap domain, or a second Gmail) for sponsor enquiries.
6. Later, add editors through **Settings → Permissions** instead of sharing your password.

## Step 3: Brand kit (days 2–7)

**Colors (use them everywhere: thumbnails, graphics, banner)**

| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Background, "the record" | Deep navy `#0B1426` |
| Signal, the future | Amber `#FFB020` |
| Text / paper | Paper white `#F4F1EA` |
| Ledger: WRONG | Red `#E5484D` |
| Ledger: RIGHT | Green `#30A46C` |

**Fonts (free, from Google Fonts):** headlines in **Space Grotesk Bold**; body and captions in **Inter**.

**Signature elements.** These are what makes the channel recognizable at a glance. Put them in every video:
- A **year stamp** in the top-left corner of every scene (e.g. `2035`) in amber Space Grotesk
- A **timeline ribbon**: a thin amber line that moves left to right as a scene transition
- **Ledger cards**: a paper-white card with `LEDGER #0001`, the claim, the source, the check date and a status stamp (OPEN / RIGHT / PARTLY / WRONG)
- A **source tag** in the bottom-left on every number (e.g. `WEF 2025`)

**Assets to make (in Canva, Photopea or Figma):**

| Asset | Size | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Profile picture | 800×800 | Shows as a circle. An amber "TR" monogram or a year-stamp glyph on navy |
| Banner | 2560×1440 | Keep text inside the **1546×423** center safe area: "The future, on the record · New documentary every other Tuesday" |
| Watermark | 150×150 | A small monogram, set to show for the whole video |
| Thumbnail template | 1280×720 | Save as a reusable file. Keep it under 2 MB |
| Ledger card template | 1920×1080 | A transparent PNG for the edit |

## Step 6: Tools

Free options first. You can launch with a total cost of zero apart from a microphone.

| Job | Free | Paid upgrade |
|---|---|---|
| Research | Official reports, Google Scholar, the Wayback Machine, Claude | n/a |
| Prediction Ledger | Google Sheets (template: `ledger-template.csv`) | n/a |
| Scripts, hooks, titles | **This skill pack**: `/yt-script`, `/yt-package`, `/yt-seo` | n/a |
| Voice | **Your own voice** + Audacity; Adobe Podcast Enhance to clean it | A USB dynamic mic (e.g. Samson Q2U or Audio-Technica ATR2100x), or a licensed AI voice used consistently |
| Visuals | Canva, Figma, Blender, **NASA images** (mostly public domain), **Copernicus / Sentinel** satellite imagery, Wikimedia Commons (check each license) | Adobe Firefly / Midjourney for illustrations; After Effects for motion graphics |
| Editing | **DaVinci Resolve** (long-form), **CapCut** (Shorts) | DaVinci Resolve Studio |
| Music | YouTube Audio Library | Epidemic Sound or Artlist (licensed for YouTube) |
| Thumbnails | Photopea or Canva | Photoshop |
| Transcripts (for `/yt-edit` and `/yt-chapters`) | YouTube's auto-captions (upload unlisted, download the .srt) or Whisper | n/a |

**Narrate in your own voice if you can.** It's the strongest defense against the "inauthentic content"
policy, and a human voice is a big part of what makes a faceless channel feel trustworthy. If you use
an AI voice, use the same licensed voice every time, and keep the scripts original.
