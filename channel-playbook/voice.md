## Who I am talking to

One person: someone between 20 and 45 who keeps hearing "AI will change everything" and "by 2030…" in the news,
half-believes it, half-doesn't, and wants someone to show them the actual evidence without hype or doom.
They watch on a TV in the evening as often as on a phone.

## How I actually talk

Calm, precise, curious. Short sentences, then the occasional long one that pays off. I say "you" a lot.
I explain a number before I use it. I admit uncertainty out loud ("the forecasts disagree here, and here's why").
I'm never smug about being right, and I'm open about being wrong.

## Words I never use

game-changer · mind-blowing · insane · crazy · unbelievable · revolutionary · "dive in" · "let's get into it" ·
"in today's video" · "smash that like button" · "the future is now" · "experts say" (with no name attached) · "scientists are baffled"

## Words I do use

"on the record" · "the forecast says" · "here's the source" · "let's check" · "graded" · a specific year instead of "soon" ·
"exposed, not replaced" · "already being built"

## What I will not claim

- Any number without a named source and a date.
- Any prediction of my own presented as fact. My own views are labeled as opinion.
- Investment, legal or medical advice.
- That a forecast "will" happen. It's always "is forecast to", "is scheduled for", or "is planned for".
- Results or credentials I don't have.

## My format

- No face on camera. Illustrated motion graphics, satellite imagery, charts.
- Narration in my own voice (or one consistent licensed voice).
- Flagships run 10–30 minutes; marathons 2–3 hours, calmer and slower.
- No channel intro. The hook starts at 0:00.
- One ask at the end, never three.
- No swearing.
- The year stamp (top-left), source tags (bottom-left) and Ledger cards in every video.
